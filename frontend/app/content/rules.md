# Booster rules

These rules apply to everyone with booster access. Moderators and admins enforce them, and breaking them can lead to a fine (deducted from your next payout) or the loss of your booster access.

## Claiming an order

- Only claim an order you can **start within a few hours**. If you claim one, you start today: an order claimed "for tomorrow" earns a fine.
- **Priority orders** have to start within an hour of claiming.
- Message the customer in the order chat **within 5 minutes** of claiming, and tell them when you'll start.
- If you can't keep an order going, **release it** so another booster can pick it up, and tell the customer why in the chat.

## Talking to customers

- Be polite and helpful, and update the customer in the order chat **at least once a day** while the order is open.
- If you stop playing for more than an hour, say when you'll continue. Not showing up when you said you would is one of the worst things you can do.
- Don't write to anyone in game while you play on a customer's account.
- **Never move a customer off King Boosting.** Offering them services outside the site, or sharing your Discord or other contacts, gets you removed on the first offence.
- When support contacts you about an order, tell the truth. Hiding something makes it much worse.

## Playing on an account

- Check the order's status before every session. If the customer **paused** it, don't play until it's resumed.
- Use a VPN in the customer's region. An account locked because you played without one is your responsibility.
- Don't spend Blue Essence, Riot Points or anything else on the account unless the customer asks you to.
- If the customer chose roles or champions, play those. Tell them in the chat before you change runes or summoner spells.
- Play your active orders every day: 4 to 8 games a day is the minimum. Stalling an order is fined.
- Never write the customer's login down anywhere outside the order page.

## Finishing and getting paid

- When the order is done, mark it **completed** from the order page. The customer is told at once.
- Your share of each completed order shows on your **Earnings** page, with any fines taken off.
- Admins pay out completed orders in batches. Every payout lists the orders and fines it covers.
- A fine says why it was given. If you think one is wrong, raise it with an admin before the next payout.
